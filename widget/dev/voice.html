Details

  • Press: every button dips to 90% and springs back.
  • Start: one ring goes out from the mic, once. Bars rise in a 30 ms stagger.
  • Words fade in (140 ms). The last, still-settling words sit at 55% white and firm up when final.
  • “Getting ready” only shows if it takes over 250 ms (first use, while macOS fetches the speech model): the glyph fades in and out, and the box shows the download %.
  • One pink thing at a time: the mic while listening; on stop it hands the pink to send, which means “Enter saves”.
  • Enter stops and saves · Esc cancels · typing takes over.
